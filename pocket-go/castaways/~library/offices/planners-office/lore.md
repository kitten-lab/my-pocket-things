# lore

Main points only. Not the novels. Expand here when a fact is true enough to sit.

---

### Chester's Imports (the shoppe)
A recursive archive. A shoppe of improbable things spanning multiple timelines. This is the **front-side shop** — the one the bookstore loops keep writing. Not the same place as the import station.

### the import station
Green terminal. IO. Main-line Sam. SDK-808. This is where the shop is *gotten*, not where the antiques sit. **Not related to the front-side shop.**

### instances of Sam
- **SDK Sam** — the one at the green terminal, receiving the shop / being imported.
- **Loop Sams** — the ones inside the story cycles (bookstore, shoppe timelines). Different instances than SDK Sam.
- **You** — a different Sam than SDK. Unless you are not. The console is pretty sure you are Sam.

### the doubt
The glass will treat you as Sam. That is part of the machine. It does not settle whether you are.

### first look — assumed
At first, the colored girls are their own stories. **Kat is not Sam is not Aubel.** Each arrives in her own timing, with her own terminal. Do not collapse them for the player.

---

### deep — no one knows this at first
Do not put this on a first key. It is the myth underneath.

- Many Samanthas.
- **One endless Chester**, everywhere at once.
- Chester is a **broken recursion**: time travel invented to save a dying wife, at a **time-locked moment**.
- That is why the shoppe can span timelines, and why the loops keep happening.
- The wife / the girl is named differently in different **colored worlds**: sometimes Samantha, sometimes Mary, sometimes Kat or Cat or Cass. First, they are assumed separate. Deep, they are the same wound, renamed.
- **Why so many names:** in one timeline he wrote stories about them — adventures, or a gentle life. He used a few names, not only her original one. The colored worlds inherited the names from the stories.
- **Chester** is not his original name either. It is just the sticky one.
