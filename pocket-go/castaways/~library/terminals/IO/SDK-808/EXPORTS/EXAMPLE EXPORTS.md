# mechanic posit

*REDEFINE* **anima** to be the aspect of the self which is the "me" principle
*REDEFINE* **animus** to be the aspect of the self which is the "you" principle

**anim-A**      - a singularity, "I am A person."
**anim-US**   - a unit, "Together, I am US."

When seeking a partner, you must know yourself, that is to say - your **anima**.  Until you are able to clearly and fully see your **anima**, you will meet and create interpersonal romantic relationships with distorted partners, either reflecting or drawing out the parts of yourself you are not actively embracing.

In these events, you will sense both your **anima** and your **animus** in your partners. The **us** is never without the **a** in which powers it. Your ideal partner is made of a similar **animus** composite to your own, and so your distortion of self draws in the **us** relationship you have between these principles within you.

To culture towards the most ideal relationships, your must observe your outer relationships and seek and dispel any and all distortions which block you from expressing your full **anima**, that of you which is your expressed singularity. Once able to express near to your truest authentic self, you will find that those you draw in hold less distortions and are able to relate to you on less distorted terms.

Negative relationships do not mean *failure* - they offer instead a mirror  of the self. Whatever comes up within the relationship can then be analyzed to better see your full **anima** expression.

## theories
Full integration of the **anima** will result in the type of romantic and interpersonal relationships everyone dreams about. 

Full integration of the **anima** will result in higher levels of consciousness.
