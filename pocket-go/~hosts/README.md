the rooms.

start.md is the front door. `/` loads it. the address bar says `start`.

a folder in here is a host. the name of the folder is the name of the door. `terminal` becomes `go.terminal`. `stores` becomes `go.stores`. `library` becomes `go.library` if you want that room again. nothing is reserved.

do not make the rooms in advance. they show up when the clay needs a house. the pocket finds them.

the drawers (~librarian, ~charlie, ~readme, ~agent) live outside. they are not worlds.

`_hosts.yaml` is optional. use it to alias (a folder named THE-DIR served as `go.dir`) or to title a room. a folder still works with no line in that file.
