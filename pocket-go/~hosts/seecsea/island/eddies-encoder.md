## Eddie walked into karaoke. The Kat renamed him Kevin. Which name is a true name anyways?

{{img:eddyencoder.png}}

`C:\ALICE_BOX\charlies-toys\eddies-encoder` · Charlie's Toys · **CO.TOY-004-EEE**

A karaoke stamp machine. It mints **KVEN** by hand: three letters, a dash, three digits. Pull is a candidate on the face — not in the bag until you USE it, or OWN / type your own. Letter pool is Chester-ish (`ABSDKIQEPLWXYZMNR`), not the full alphabet, not auto-batch.


Not datBOX Core. Not Nim. Not My Pocket chrome. Gold stays in `prod/safe_box/`. Do not copy the bags here.

## Desk

Left: the stamp (PULL · USE · OWN). Right: the index of bagged slips, searchable. Open a card for the creature slip — guarded name, in-world alts, log spellings, type (person / object / event / place / other), notes, first and last occurred — and a tape strip of links to other KVENs. Kind of link grows as you invent kinds (`relates_to`, `aka_of`, `from_log_as`, …).

Two minds, same layout: karaoke desk or dark angry, from the Deck menu.

Deck Host only. Port **43145**. Glass **800×600**. `run-eddie.bat` from `prod/`. Never Chrome as home.
