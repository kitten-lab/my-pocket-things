Over the course of the next 18 months she would become my everything friend, center of my days, nanny to my children, to family, to someone both my husband and I loved and wanted to care for, to a woman we were both literally in love with, to making her whole family mine including her husband to... 165k in debt, deep damage to my 20 year relationship, fractures in my children's childhoods creating anxiety and trauma. Yelling. Accusations. From heaven to hell.

Older people, my parents generation, say "well, you've learned your lesson" and while I find this to feel like victim blaming, there must be something to find. It was July 8th I kicked them out of my house and set firm boundaries, and as such it has been almost 6 months I have contemplated this question. What do I learn? And the depth of that lesson is still unfolding.

I will grow from this, as all before it.
