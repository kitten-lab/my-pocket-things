[purple shooting star]

dark;
is it external or internal?
does it matter where the sun is in the sky?
shadows fall behind everything the light shines on

So the lightness itself creates shadow. If we stand in the light, we will cast a shadow: Are we the cause or is the light? It is just the interaction of the 2 together, and even then is anything at fault?

When the light shines on me, was she the shadow? The what could have been? My maladapted result, what could have been produced had I been weaker, less able to handle the pressures of a childhood marked by fracture after fracture?

Thick. As my hate and darkness increased as she was here, what part was me feeling her shadow, the true darkness and emptiness. Was it all hers or is some of it mine?

I learn from the dark. When I was young, and still now. We have range. We of the light cast shadows. It is nature.
