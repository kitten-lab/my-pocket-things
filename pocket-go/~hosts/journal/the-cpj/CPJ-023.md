what does love mean? It is a word that applied means many different things to different people. Reminds how we are all in a dream constructed by our society, our families, our schools and our churches, and especially our experiences. So what is love? Its so unclear we try to subset it, conditional love, unconditional love, "love-love", love like a friend, a mother's love. BOUNDLESS LOVE, it seems ephemeral, its tangible in concept until you try to produce it on paper and even then, it is clouded by those perceptions. But the Divine Mother, the purest of light, the hope after all is lost, stands to heal the soul so deeply.. Is that love? I believe God is love, nature is love, all is love.

But what constitutes LOVE?
