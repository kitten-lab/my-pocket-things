When we say let there be light, what are we saying? Let there be vision. Let us be able to see the shades below, find what is hidden, illuminate the mind, transcend all things into purity. Before there was light, what was there? Was everything there, waiting to be seen? Was it created only when light touched it? What is inside black matter? Is the universe expanding? Contracting? Are we in an infinite loop, a breathe of life so big that everything else is contained in it? And always, does it matter to me, ADAM, human, one of many who walk forward blindly but with my hands on the walls because why? Am I afraid to fall? Why has trust and knowing become so hard? I read our goal is to become "one" with Nature, God, True Self, Light in the end. This is the road I chose and I choose to travel. Point one - what is light? what is perception? Everything is dark when you look at the shadow side.
