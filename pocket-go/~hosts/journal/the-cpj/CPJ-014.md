The Story of HER cannot be captured in one entry, but will likely affect me for the rest of life. So I will try to unfold it here, to remember, to dissect and to continue to understand the complex prism that is the lesson she was sent to teach me.

So, she seemed to be a great match for friendships, something I was seeking. I had found some, but my husband's introversion made it a struggle. I had a reliance on him: emotionally, as my soul "true" friend, but also physically as I had no way to drive myself around. Somehow, I run into this woman out at the coffee shop and she invites my family to a Gala for the organization that her daughter was gifted trip from: They were going to be the feature family of that year's event. Cornered yet feeling open to new experiences, I considered it. A few days later, we were speaking often through text messages. She suggested my daughter should come with them to have custom dresses made, the dress maker was donating her time and she wanted a "friend" to come along. Again, I said yes.
