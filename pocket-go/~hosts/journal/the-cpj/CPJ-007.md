[waxing crescent mark]
Dec 28 2019

I found an old journal from 20 years ago.
it is not the first time I have found it over
those years, but it was the first time I
had actually sat down to read it without
cringing self consciously at the teenage
drama sure to stain the pages. Big sticker
on the cover screaming "Popularity is
a Socially Transmitted Disease" just doesn't
yell deep, does it? This time the journal
went lost [crossed out] so I feared it had been taken
during "The Event" I am sure I will
cover in these pages.

Finding it again brought me joy and
I set about reading it. What I learned
was while yes, I lament like a teenager
I also was filled with loving-kindness,
deep desire to find connection amid
a sea of toxic relationships, from
family to friends to lovers, and of
course, the relationship I had with
the "self" inside of me. But another
layer is this: I found that who I am
in the pages. My dabbling obsessions
with mysticism, religion unification,
the rights to be who you are and love
what you love - those truths were
there. Unashamed rambles, dark
poetry, cute doodles, seeking connections
