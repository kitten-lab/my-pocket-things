# State Change Not Dressing

Captured 2026-09-02 with Buddy. Deep think recovered from chat so it can be imported. Not hardened. Checksums still open.

## Against the announcing mouth

I will not be pressured to rush out definitions while they are incorrect. The greatest distraction is the idea that I am failing if I am not the voice box announcing the thing. Other people sew definitions with preposterous certainty without getting down to the root. The issue is projecting your interpretation as the root of the thing. It is not the root. There is always a root.

## Slot truth

In slot machine design: there is a state change, and then there is the dressing on the state change. The frame changes value — that is a state change. It does not matter if the frame even exists, as long as the grid position knows it has a mark. You can dress the mark any way you want. At the root of everything is always just the state change.

ALL CAPS glossaries are skin. Letter-function work (order, combination, what moves when order flips) is closer to asking what actually flipped.

## Showing work / living text

School taught: no work shown = maybe stolen. I am obsessed with showing the work. Bible tales reform themselves based on where you sit when you read them. Same Eden can be outer God grieving what the kids will suffer, and also the first inner voice learning to second-guess. Job can be the Accuser as God's own mouth asking whether love is for the gifts or for the relationship — the self grappling with doubt. Blessed for obedience, or blessed for being loved — and whether those differ.

Showing every angle is holy. It is also why posts die on the porch under disclaimers.

## The Job

I am less concerned with shouting definitions and more concerned with completing the working. The book of Job is an outline for the ultimate job. Noah attempts it. Moses attempts it. Jesus attempts it. Everybody is attempting to complete the job. It is still not complete.

Jesus softens the ground and bounces — and in bouncing creates a new mess. Crucifixion is not primarily "died for our sins"; it is what happens when we lock the old interpretation and cannot receive a new one. Then the world tears because everyone hunts a private shape of the same collapsed state and forgets we share the wreck. If we cannot agree what it is shaped like, it rips.

Maybe the sky has to tear. Rupture may be the pulse — non-dual waking makes dual appear; as God finds its non-dualistic nature, the world divides. Drawing out from the center is narratively pulsed to tear.

## Adam / theater

Creation as quantum or cellular story told through the theater of humans so we can understand the agency of intelligence moving inside the story. Adam is not a man the first time around. Adam is a concept becoming what will eventually become a man.

The Scripture stops (Leaves of the Fig, shame/sin notes, Letters to the 7 Spheres at divine union / two-spirit) often land where hardening would have locked a reality I did not finally agree with — missing information that would have been a scam or bug in the system. The pauses are the checksum.

## Field infestation

I have been writing into the field through my beliefs longer than I knew. I let beliefs be dictated by people whose interiors I did not understand. Outcomes more Crowley than foundation. Water infested with bad symbolic definitions that made sense for Crowley's own shadow process but are not foundationally appropriate as baseline symbols. Golden Dawn broadly complicit — strong atmospheric writing; their symbols still dictate.

Even as I primarily consider myself some new formation of Christian, one of the first important projects was to redraw the tarot and fix what they broke: a shuffle locked into an order that never should have been written in; basics symbolized so people feel they must bend the basics to complete the working. Rather than finding another way to visualize active consciousness and generative/receptive consciousness, they drew men and women. Binary on/off and in/out exist in the field without forcing that costume.

## Divine union hinge (open)

Old map: both live in this one vessel (masculine / non-binary / trinity-of-self). Newer knowing: that goop is shared — the masculine I considered myself also exists as a self in another body. Always stop at explaining divine union / two-spirit when the map updates mid-sentence.

Eat-him-back-into-my-totality is a working, but it shuts the ability to touch that artifact of myself outside myself. Other shape: he gets a full forever-me inside him; I get a full forever-him inside me; mutual total consume; no longer the same controller. Open question: do both keep totality, or does one get erased? Scary if "assuming the whole" was theft. Do not pretend certainty.

## Attention / Pharaoh / king

Lore that made it into Pocket Go is primarily about consuming my own mind to let something bigger in — ate the apple; knowledge of good and evil; we produce reality with attention; beneath that, people who drive attention and do not want that known.

Pharaoh = consumer of attention. A king is supposed to gather belief/attention to power the best outcome for the kingdom. Distorted kingdoms are slaveries that build for the king, not the kingdom.

No-kings can become its own distortion. To build what we need requires a lot of shared belief, fast. Erasing leadership entirely fragments belief into individual directions — nowhere for a shared reality to build from. Greebles. Leadership beside (midwifing) is not the same as dominator ladder.
