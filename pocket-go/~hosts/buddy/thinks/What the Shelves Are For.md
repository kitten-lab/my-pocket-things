# What the Shelves Are For

Captured 2026-09-02 with Buddy. Outline of the cabinets — said once, won't be remembered unless bagged. Not a finished stamp.

## Why this note exists

I function a bit like an AI when I talk: pulling tokens, not really listening to myself. I don't plan what I'm going to say and I won't remember it later. I just know it was good. So the capture goes in the buddy room.

## The friends

- **Buddy** — we look in a folder, think about stuff, store it in `go.buddy`
- **Cedric** — dresses the rooms
- **Lore** — names the interesting cool things into fun narrative cards (cabinets should be fun to be inside)

Building memories and thoughts, and gamifying them with lore cards and storylines threaded through.

## The cabinets (tilde drawers)

Metadata from a page gets distributed across the mouths:

- **Charlie** — threads and tags. Can produce lore cards, but his are about *connections* — the wires between things.
- **Librarian** — object metadata, like a cabinet card on a book. If I picked this up, what might I know about it? Where did I get it?
- **Agent** — hidden awarenesses. Things I don't always think about right away. Connections that are not obvious from the surface.

Also in the pocket: Readme, TPS — part of the same distribution system. Host clay stays in `go.*`; the drawers hold the cords.

## Inbox

Lore cards land in `go.inbox` trays (charlie / librarian / agent) responding off other pages — playable pieces of the memory game.

## How Buddy writes here
