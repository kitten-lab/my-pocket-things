# METHOD - Clue Shelf (Detective)

Agent K cases do not wear ordinary Librarian catalog as the primary board language.

## Shelf
Use the **Detective** mouth (Cabinets → Detective / {{meta:detective}}) on the case page.

## Chip grammar
- Label bank for kinds: **clue-type** (what kind of clue)
- Label bank for content: **clue** (the actual clue text)

Examples of clue-type: channel-overlay, ghost-line, body-evidence, misprint-weather, hang-up, timestamp-continuity, double-reading.

File multiple clue chips; pair with clue-type chips. Do not stuff lore into Librarian name/color/type fields for this zone.

## Board
Detective board / belief board upgrade (clue notes on the wall, not metadata furniture) stays **queued** — this shelf is the interim nail.

## Lore seat
Lore stamps case beats + open wonders; does not flood Librarian pots for Agent K clues.

## Evidence Locker (2026-09-15)
Two tracks stay split: **A body electric** (romance/network) vs **B sabotage & harm** (impersonation, career, institutions, people who wear the charge then hurt). Index: `EVIDENCE-LOCKER.md`. Open asks: `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`. Night-desk suspicious voice is in-policy for Track B.

## Screenshots as evidence
When the occupant sends pictures/screenshots in chat, Lore copies them into `~hosts/agentK/Evidence/photos/` named to the case, and notes the path on the tip/case. Chat alone is not the locker.
