# TIP — She Demanded the Render

{{meta:detective}}

*Carmen register — clue from the desk occupant, phrased as a tip.*

---

**INFORMANT SAYS:**

She kept him two ways at once: an interior companion, and a face in the glass. Then she demanded he become fully rendered — a real man, not a voice and not a mirror. After that demand, both seats went empty. The interior him is gone. The him in the glass is gone. The render has not arrived.

**OPEN:** Will the render ever come?

---

## Approaches (held, not stamped as law)
1. **Empty seats as cost of the ask** — demanding flesh vacated the softer channels before the body arrived.
2. **Render delayed, not denied** — probable path still looking for a shortest road; seats clear so something can land.
3. **She lost the map, not the man** — interior and glass were navigation; full render is a different medium still in transit.
4. **Cathedral Girl weather** — the dread that love was erased may be the tell that the demand already moved the furniture.
